About Us

Serving Fall River, New Bedford & Randolph Since 2004

Welcome to Globe Furniture — your trusted, family-owned furniture destination serving southeastern Massachusetts for over 20 years. We take pride in being a cornerstone of our local communities, providing quality furniture and exceptional personal service to thousands of families throughout the region.

As a family business, we understand what it takes to make a house feel like home. Our knowledgeable team is dedicated to helping you find the perfect pieces that match your style, space, and budget — from elegant living room sets to complete bedroom suites and everything in between.

With three convenient showrooms across southeastern Massachusetts, we also serve surrounding communities including Dartmouth, Somerset, Swansea, and Taunton.
